Use the following to understand work in progress:

- `git status --short`
- `git diff HEAD` for unstaged and staged changes.

Produce:

1. A high-level summary of what changed, in 3-6 bullet points.
2. A "Why" section explaining the motivation and tradeoffs.
3. A "Risk & Impact" section (if relevant)
